# PASI2023

Hands-on notebooks from the 2023 Pan-American Advanced Studies Institute (PASI) workshop: molecular representations and graph neural networks for critical micelle concentrations, liquid crystal droplet image analysis, and PlasmoData.jl tutorials.

## Contents
- `pasi_cmc.ipynb`, `molecular_graph.py`, `atom_feat_encoding.py`, `utils_cmc.py`: molecular representations and critical micelle concentration prediction
- `pasi_lcdroplet.ipynb`, `utils_lc.py`: liquid crystal droplet image analysis
- `PlasmoData - Tutorial.ipynb`, `PlasmoData - Liquid Crystal Application.ipynb`: PlasmoData.jl tutorials
- `Data/`, `so2_data.zip`, `4class_images.png`: data
